Yapling · Clickable prototype: the first week on iPhone (day 1 to day 8)

Rounds 4 and 5 (with the polish fixes) are the source of truth: same Sticker + Glass style, same cast, same components. Don't redesign anything. Turn the screens into one clickable iPhone prototype of a learner's first week, so we can put it in the hands of 5 to 8 Vietnamese working professionals and watch them use it. It should feel like the real app: animated critters, voices you can hear, the burrow changing day by day. It doesn't need to be a real AI.

What we're testing: do people understand that Pip is a roommate learning English from them, do they actually speak out loud, does each day feel new, do they want to come back tomorrow, and does the paywall feel fair.

How it works:
- iPhone 17 frame (402×874), runs in Safari on a real iPhone when opened full screen. One continuous path with a few branches; every screen has a way forward and a way back.
- Language: app chrome, permissions, paywall and safety in Vietnamese (with an EN switch for our team). Learning content stays in English with tap-to-reveal Vietnamese, exactly as in round 5.
- Voices: character lines play out loud (use the browser's speech voice, a different one per character, with captions) and the critter's mouth moves with the caption timing. Pip's calls keep the burrow softly blurred behind; Mei's call happens behind the café counter.
- Speaking is simulated: the tester holds the mic, speaks out loud and lets go. The prototype shows a believable caption of their answer and the scene carries on. Where the browser supports speech recognition, show what they actually said instead.
- Facilitator panel, hidden behind a three-finger tap (or a small strip outside the phone frame on desktop): jump to any day, choose how the next answer lands (fine / makes the target error / goes silent), toggle "came back after 3 days away", switch VI/EN, reset. Testers should never see it.
- Between days, show the iOS lock screen with that morning's notification. Tapping it opens the app on that day.

The week (matches Season 1, Episode 1 "The Letter"):

Day 1 · Moving in
1. Splash, pick first language (Tiếng Việt), "Pip is an AI" explainer, choose your roommate (Pip the red panda; capybara and cat shown as other options).
2. Day-one burrow: boxes everywhere, one shelf. Pip: "Help me unpack?" The unpacking chat is the one-minute placement (3 or 4 simple questions about the user's job and day).
3. Residency card: "Newcomer, welcome to Natterwick", three things they already do well, the first two can-dos, and "Too easy? Too hard?"
4. Notifications asked in context ("Want Pip to call when something happens in town?"), with quiet hours.
5. Linh knocks with a welcome. At dusk, through the burrow window, a tiny animation of a letter pushed under the café door.

Day 2 · The Letter (Episode call, about 4 minutes)
Notification: "Pip: Something's going on at the café. Come with me?"
6. The week view: ring with three empty slots (Episode, Real week, Practice) and this week's four phrases as seed packets: What's wrong? · I need a plan · Can I help? · I think you should…
7. The call with Mei and Pip, beat by beat from the script: Pip whispers "Can you ask her what's wrong?", Mei deflects then shows the letter (tap a line for Vietnamese, which drops a seed packet), Pip asks "What does rent mean? Is it a kind of bread?", the call to the landlord's office (introduce yourself), one piece of advice, Pip announces "I'll get a job!"
8. One correction prompt in the call: Pip repeats the error back as a question, the user says it again, then "fixed" sparkles. Also show the stuck path: after 4 seconds of silence a phrase hint slides up.
9. "What does that mean?" chip once in the call: simple English first, Vietnamese one more tap away.
10. Post-call card: two wins, one fix, phrases moving from seed to sprout. Ring fills 1 of 3. A burrow box gets unpacked.

Day 3 · Something new without a call
Notification: "Pip got lost today. It has a story for you."
11. Pip's voice story (40 seconds, it asked a seagull for directions) with captions you can tap, and a postcard from Linh ("When you forget a word, describe it"). Nothing to fail, no live minutes used. A new object appears in the burrow.

Day 4 · Real week (about 3 minutes)
Notification: "Pip: Can you teach me how to introduce myself at work?"
12. Pip asks how people introduce themselves at work; the user introduces their own job in 30 seconds; Pip tries it back using the user's words; one more try with a timer ring. Ring fills 2 of 3.

Day 5 · Pass it on (Practice call, about 4 minutes)
13. Bea's Bus 45: tell the same story about your day to Pip (90 s), Linh (60 s), then Bea (45 s). After each round the fluency mirror shows Flow, Pace and Pauses with units, and the third round is visibly smoother.
14. Ring full: the week keepsake, and the first can-do keepsake drawn by Pip and hung on the burrow wall ("Introduced myself in 30 seconds · witnessed by Linh").

Day 6 · The cliffhanger
Notification: "Pip left you a voicemail."
15. Pip's voicemail: "Big news! Hollowbrook is hiring. I have an interview on Thursday. Can you help me practise?" Then Pip reuses a phrase slightly wrong ("Can I helping you?") and the user fixes it; the phrase becomes a leaf. Season map with Episode 2 glowing.

Day 7 · The end of the free week
16. A calm, honest paywall in Vietnamese: the first week was on us; from now, Free is about 10 live minutes a week plus every story, postcard and voicemail; Plus is about 150 minutes a month (₫149k/month, ₫799k/year, ₫599k first year, student price). "Not now" is as easy to tap as "Try Plus", and Pip, the burrow and the story stay either way.

Day 8 · Coming back
17. Normal return: "How was your week?" and a peek at Episode 2. The "came back after 3 days" version: Pip is happy to see them, tells the recap line, and the episode is exactly where they left it. No guilt anywhere, no sad critter, no lost progress.

Also include: no connection ("Pip's line is crackly, try a voice note"), mic denied, "Sorry, I didn't catch that, say it another way?", and the safety sheet with 115, each reachable from the facilitator panel.

Deliver it as one prototype file plus a one-page map of every screen and branch.
