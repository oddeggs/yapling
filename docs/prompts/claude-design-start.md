Yapling: speak English with a critter who's learning it from you (iOS/Android, portrait)

An AI English speaking app for young professionals and students who can read English but freeze when they have to speak it. Your roommate is a yapling: a critter newcomer who just moved to Natterwick, a cosy harbour town where critters from everywhere live, so everyone speaks English with a different accent. Your yapling learns English from you, and you rehearse your real life together in live voice calls with lipsync: job interviews, standups, client calls, small talk. Every place in town is a scenario (café, market, office, interview room, inn, bus stop, town square), and every neighbour is a competent professional with their own English: Singaporean, Indian, British, American, Filipino, Australian. Something new happens in town every time you open the app, weekly story episodes end on cliffhangers you resolve by speaking, and nothing ever dies.

Style: clean, polished and premium iOS 27 natively looking, with assets crafted with https://github.com/alesha-pro/tools/tree/main/skills/hand-drawn-canvas-animation skill in a flat-vector 2D with the character craft of Duolingo, but warmer and cosier so it never reads as a Duolingo clone: think cosy-game village rather than classroom. Bold rounded shapes, a warm daylight palette with one confident accent, big expressive eyes and brows. Grown-up charm, not a kids' app. No hand-drawn texture; crisp and friendly. Characters built from separable parts (head, body, eyes, brows, mouth) for Rive.

Motion: character-led and bouncy, like Rive state machines. Real-time lipsync from an 8-shape mouth set (rest, A/I, E, O, U, M/B/P, F/V, L/TH). The yapling listens with a patient pose and tilts its head while thinking, so there's never dead air. A ringing incoming call that wiggles the whole screen. Word objects pop into the burrow with squash and stretch when you use a new phrase, then sprout, leaf and bloom as you master it. A moving van rolls in when a new neighbour arrives. A town-square celebration with confetti when you report a real-life win. Haptics on calls, new words and milestones. Nothing guilt-inducing: no sad pets, no red badges, no streak flames. Sound effect and music compose using https://github.com/Leonxlnx/earshot/ skill.

Features:
- Live calls: voice conversations with your yapling or a neighbour, patient turn-taking, a "hold on, I'm thinking" button, captions you can hide.
- Teach the yapling: it misunderstands a word and you explain it another way; it uses your phrases back days later.
- Bring your homework: paste a job description, agenda or client email and the town turns it into a rehearsal.
- Pass it on: tell a story three times as the listeners' time shrinks, ending with the bus driver who has 45 seconds.
- The burrow: your home, filled with objects that grew from words you've spoken.
- Story seasons: weekly episodes in town ("The Café Might Close"); missed weeks wait for you.
- Kind notifications: incoming calls, cliffhanger voicemails, "can you help me practise for my interview?"
- Growth mirror: a monthly voice time capsule (day 1 vs today) with friendly stats: Flow, Pace, Pauses.
- Weekly ring: three calls fill it; missed weeks pause it, nothing breaks.
- Memory page: everything your yapling remembers about you, editable and deletable.
- First run: pick a starter (round-eared hedgehog, sleek otter, tufted fox kit), name it, and have your first tiny chat in under a minute, with no tutorial wall.

Screens: onboarding and starter pick, the burrow home (the hero screen), incoming call, the in-call screen, the post-call card ("3 things Pip learned from you", new word objects, one fluency number), the town map, Bring your homework, the monthly time capsule, the memory page and the Plus paywall.
