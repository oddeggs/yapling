Yapling · Round 6: the beginner path ("Settling in") and three call fixes

Keep the style, cast, type and every existing screen exactly as they are (Sticker + Glass, ink-outlined stickers, lilac/cyan/mint, iOS liquid glass, Lexend + the hand-written font). This round adds screens for true beginners (A0–A1) and fixes three things testers struggled with in calls. All copy below is final unless marked; Vietnamese first where the user's language shows.

**The idea.** A real beginner couldn't get through week 1. Before the story starts, beginners now play six short calls ("Settling in" / "Làm quen") where Linh, the pangolin next door who also came from Vietnam, helps you and Pip settle in after the ferry: 1 Hello · 2 Boxes · 3 Again, please · 4 Bus 45 (Bea) · 5 Mei's café · 6 About me. Each call is 2–3 minutes. The character always says their own answer first, then you read one of two cards aloud. Nothing fails, nothing is timed, and the words "level", "beginner" and "fail" never appear. The new lowest stage is called **Visitor · Khách mới đến**.

Screens to add:

1. **The level question** (onboarding, a new step after the AI explainer, before picking a roommate). "Tiếng Anh của bạn bây giờ thế nào?" Two large chips: **"Mình mới bắt đầu"** (sub: chào hỏi, nói tên, vài từ) and **"Mình nói được một chút"** (sub: giới thiệu bản thân, gọi món). Linh peeks in, friendly, not a quiz.
2. **Visitor residency card.** The existing residency card with a different stamp: "Visitor · Khách mới đến", the line "Linh sẽ giúp bạn làm quen trước", the first two can-dos ("Say hello and your name", "Ask someone to say it again"), and no "Chặng N/5" number. Buttons: **"Làm quen với Linh"** (primary) and **"Vào thẳng truyện"** (secondary).
3. **The burrow during Settling in.** The day-one burrow with boxes; one box unpacks per finished chapter, so the room fills as you learn. A chapter card ("Làm quen · 2/6 · Boxes", Linh's sticker) with the day's button. After two chapters in a day: **"Để mai"** with **"Làm tiếp"** beside it. A small "Settling in" list (six chapters, done ones ticked, replay any) with **"Vào truyện luôn"** at the bottom. After chapter 6: dusk, the letter under Mei's café door (the existing moment).
4. **A Settling-in call** (Linh at the burrow door, chapter 1), in the existing call layout. Show:
   - **The say-this card**, shown from the start above the mic: the English to say ("Nice to meet you, too.") with the Vietnamese under it in the hand-written font, and a small speaker icon to hear it.
   - **Two-choice variant**: two cards side by side ("Tea, please." / "Coffee, please."), each with its Vietnamese; read either aloud.
   - **Two small glass pills** above the mic: **"Nói lại"** (say it again) and **"Chậm thôi"** (slower). These work in every call, not only for beginners.
   - **A Vietnamese task card** at the start of a new kind of task: "Nghe số, rồi chọn thẻ và đọc to."
   - **"Bỏ qua"**, a quiet pill that appears after one try.
   - A passed turn: the card ticks softly and Pip copies you ("Hi! Hi, Linh!").
5. **Hold-to-talk, redesigned for everyone.** Testers tapped the mic instead of holding it. Show the mic being held with a ring that fills over 2 seconds (ready to talk when full), and two gentle retry tips: after a short tap, **"Giữ nút trong lúc nói"**; after a hold with no words heard, **"Mình chưa nghe rõ. Giữ nút và nói lại nhé."** Nothing red, nothing that looks like an error.
6. **No timers while you think.** Remove any countdown from the call. After a long quiet, the hint card may slide up once on its own, silently; that's the only change. Show the call waiting calmly.
7. **"What was said" (every call).** Testers missed lines when a character said several in a row. Show the current speech bubble with a small "2/3" and a way back to the earlier lines of this turn, and a pull-up sheet listing the call's lines so far (speaker sticker, English, tap for Vietnamese, a small replay icon). Every line in a call can show its Vietnamese with a tap.
8. **"Khó quá" sheet** (from the call's exit sheet, the residency card and the Me sheet). Two choices: **"Nói chậm hơn"** (stay, slower voices) and **"Tập với Linh trước"** (practise with Linh first; the scene saves its place). Copy: "Linh sẽ tập cùng bạn vài buổi ngắn. Quán của Mei vẫn chờ bạn."
9. **Visitor after-call card.** "Bạn đã nói được:" then the learner's own sentences as little stickers ("Hi!" "I'm Lan." "Nice to meet you, too."), the phrase seed packet for the chapter, and a one-tap "Vừa sức / Khó quá". No score, no fixes.
10. **Stage-up keepsake** (end of week 1 for a Visitor). Linh hands you your Newcomer residency card at your door: "You're not a visitor any more." Buttons: **"Nhận thẻ"** and **"Ở lại thêm"** (stay a Visitor a bit longer).
11. **Me sheet rows**: a stage row showing Visitor, "Làm quen lại với Linh" (replay Settling in) and "Thử nhanh hơn".

Make a board for each screen with its states, on iPhone, plus a short flow strip: level question → Visitor card → chapter 1 call → after-call card → burrow with a box unpacked.
