Natterwick · the places

‘Twelve Small Paintings’

Every place in town is its own painting, painted live from your clock and the weather, with a neighbour who keeps their own hours and a piece of music that belongs only there. Change the time or the weather above and the whole town changes with it. Each place has three small stories you can turn to.

One field, one redEach place has its own colour field and exactly one small red thing: a neon 45, a lantern, a second hand, a sock.
Neighbours keep hoursMei opens at 7. Hank eats at the stall after dark. Nell folds towels at the laundromat from 8 till 2. Out of hours, the place says so.
Weather is lightRain greys the sky and turns lamps into long reflections; fog swallows the stars and widens the lighthouse beam; snow brightens the ground. Indoors, it's at the window.
Every place sounds like itselfA brushed jazz trio at the café, a cello and bell buoys at the harbour, a tumble-dryer beat at the laundromat. Made with ElevenLabs, mixed with the weather.
Painted in code from a light model with nine keyframes through the day; the neighbours are the same rigs as the cast sheet, lit by each place's own lamps. Tap a painting to nudge its resident.
