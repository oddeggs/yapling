The album
The burrow shows what you can say; the album shows what you've found. Every prop and place you meet lands here, a shelf per topic and a page per place. Rarity is a sticker treatment, earned only by playing: common (white die-cut), rare (foil, from a twist or a milestone) and seasonal (gold glow, only during Natterwick's own festivals, back next year if you miss it). Bought items never appear here.
